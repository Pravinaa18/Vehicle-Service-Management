Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 19, 2026

At AutoCare ("we", "our", or "us"), we prioritize your privacy and are committed to safeguarding the personal information and vehicle diagnostic records entrusted to our management platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to deliver premier automotive servicing, including your name, email address, telephone number, vehicle specifications (make, model, year, VIN/license registration, odometer mileage readings), and service histories.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used strictly to process service bookings, assign specialized mechanics, notify you of live vehicle milestones (e.g., inspection completed, ready for pickup), generate digital invoices, and preserve lifelong maintenance histories.

3. Data Protection and Security

We deploy robust cryptographic password hashing (Werkzeug / PBKDF2), secure session control, parameter-sanitized database queries, and restricted role-based authorizations to ensure your private vehicle records are secure.

4. Contact Support

If you have any questions regarding your data or account privacy, contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@autocare.com.
